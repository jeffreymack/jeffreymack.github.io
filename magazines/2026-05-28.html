Gartner's new research, covered this week by Fortune, drops a number that should stop every AI vendor's pitch cold: nearly $40 billion invested in generative AI, and 95% of organizations are getting zero return. Eight in ten senior executives say AI has had no measurable impact on either employment or productivity. Productivity compounded; organizational knowledge was retained; AI fluency increased across teams. This is the model that works—and it's still the minority approach in most enterprises.
